        Trainees

        Each student joins one program. Every shift in a production unit adds to their production score. When the score reaches the program's target, the trainer issues a certificate with the training profile.

        Production score is hours worked plus units made. The target is 400 for handicraft, tailoring and cleaning materials, and 240 for fundamentals of computers, which is training only and sends nothing to the store.

        Store and dispatch

        Everything the production units make goes into the store. Stock leaves only when it is sent to a school, and each dispatch is written to the ledger.

          How much to make

          Each school's use over the last three months, scaled to its staff today, gives next month's demand. Whatever the store cannot cover, plus a buffer, is what the production units need to make.

          Demand by school

          Forecast = average monthly use over the last three months × (staff now ÷ staff when that use was measured). Staff means teaching and non-teaching staff. Change a headcount to see the forecast move.
